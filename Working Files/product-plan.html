Product Plan

Australian Payroll Association

A lead-capture CRM for membership, training & consulting — built so no lead goes unseen.

What we're actually building and why

A CRM that puts every new membership, training, and consulting lead in one view the moment it arrives — so a lead can never sit unseen and untouched again. That's the exact gap you named: today, nobody has a single view of where the new leads are.

Who it's for: you as the operator (and your sales team working the pipeline), plus the person filling out the form on payrollgroup.com.au — someone weighing membership, training, or a consulting engagement with the Association.
What we are deliberately NOT building: no affiliates, no subscriptions, no cohorts, no analytics dashboards, no integrations beyond what's named here. If it isn't load-bearing for capturing and working a lead, it's out.

The brief that drives every build

Two surfaces: a public marketing site that captures leads, and an /admin CRM. /admin is open at first and gets locked down with email-and-password Supabase Auth later — no auth until you explicitly ask.

The CRM has four parts, all accessed server-side with the Supabase service key (secrets never reach the browser):

People
Contact directory, one row per person, deduplicated by email. Custom attributes live in a jsonb field.
Contacts
Inquiry pipeline. Each inquiry links to a person and moves through new_lead → contacted → discovery_call → proposal → won / lost.
activity_log
Every status change on a Contacts row writes one row here.
Orders
What people bought — product, amount, status.
Newsletter
Not a table — tracked by people.ok_to_contact = true.
Build 1 · Small

Prove the loop

Goal: A stranger can submit an inquiry on your live site and you can see that lead inside /admin, on the same day, without anyone touching the database by hand. This closes the exact gap you named. It's the smallest thing that proves the whole system works end to end — nothing else matters until this is real.

Scope

The People and Contacts tables with custom attributes in the jsonb column; a working contact form on the live site that writes a People row (upserted by email) and a linked Contacts row; one admin login with a single verified account; one admin page listing incoming leads newest first.

Definition of Done

  • The contact form is live on your real domain, not localhost.
  • Submitting it creates exactly one People row and one linked Contacts row, deduplicated by email on repeat submits.
  • Your chosen custom attributes are saved correctly inside attributes.
  • A new Contacts row lands in status new_lead.
  • You can log in to /admin with your one seeded account.
  • The admin leads page shows the submission within seconds, newest first.
  • You personally run the full flow once: submit as a visitor, log in, see it.

Success Criteria

  • From a cold start, you can go submit → visible in under 60 seconds.
  • Two submissions from the same email produce one person, not two.
  • You can read the lead's name, type, message, and custom attributes on the admin page without opening Supabase.
  • No lead can land and go unseen — the failure mode you named.
Build 2 · All

Make it the system you run the business from

Goal: Turn the proven loop into the place you actually manage relationships and money. You work leads, record what people bought, and keep your newsletter list entirely from /admin behind your login — and every new lead gets an automatic confirmation email. This is what makes your 90-day win achievable.

Scope

The rest of the /admin back end behind your login: the full People directory, all inquiries with working pipeline stages, the Orders list, and the Newsletter list (ok_to_contact = true). Plus Resend wired so a confirmation email fires on form submit. Every Contacts status change writes an activity_log row.

Definition of Done

  • All four parts (People, Contacts, Orders, Newsletter) are visible and usable in /admin, and all of /admin sits behind your login.
  • You can move a Contacts row through new_lead → contacted → discovery_call → proposal → won / lost from the interface.
  • Each status change writes one activity_log row with from_status, to_status, and actor.
  • The People directory is searchable and shows your custom attributes.
  • You can add an Orders row against a person and see it on their record.
  • The Newsletter list shows everyone with ok_to_contact = true.
  • Resend is connected, the sending domain is verified, and a real confirmation email arrives after a form submit.

Success Criteria

  • You can run a lead from first inquiry to won without leaving /admin or touching the database.
  • A person's full history — inquiries, status changes, and orders — is visible in one place.
  • A test submission produces a confirmation email in the inbox, not spam, with your domain as the sender.
  • Nothing in /admin is reachable without logging in.
  • At ~40 inquiries a week, this keeps up without you dropping to the database by hand.
